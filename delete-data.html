Delete your XO Journey data

XO Journey (Android game on Google Play)

How to ask us to delete your data

  1. Open XO Journey and go to Multiplayer → Friends. Your Player ID is shown at the top (for example XO-48291).
  2. Send an email to arshadh6611@gmail.com with the subject "XO Journey data deletion" and your Player ID.
  3. We delete your online data within 30 days and reply to let you know it is done.

No account or password is needed. If you never opened Multiplayer, nothing about you is stored on our server.

What is deleted

  • Your Player ID and its secret key
  • Your name and avatar
  • Your friends list and pending friend requests
  • Your online wins and XP on the leaderboard

Data on your phone

Your progress, coins and settings are stored only on your phone. You can remove all of it yourself: uninstall the game, or open your phone's Settings → Apps → XO Journey → Storage → Clear data.

What is kept

  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play (not by us), as required for payments and refunds.
  • Anonymous statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase) are not linked to your Player ID and are deleted automatically by Google after their retention period (up to 14 months).
  • Nothing else is kept after deletion.

Privacy policy: see the XO Journey privacy policy on this site.
