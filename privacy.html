Privacy Policy

ZenEcho 3D: ASMR Puzzle Box (Android package com.shafique.joki) · Last updated: September 26, 2026

Summary

ZenEcho 3D is an offline puzzle game. There are no accounts and no server, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. The third parties are Google AdMob (optional ads, from level 8), Google Play (optional token purchases) and Google Firebase (anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, which you can turn off).

Data stored on your device

  • level progress, stars and best moves
  • your token balance
  • settings (nature sounds, sound effects, volumes, vibration)
  • small app flags: the day of your last daily gift, and whether the welcome message and the tutorial were shown

This data stays on your phone and is not sent to us.

Usage statistics and crash reports

To make the game better (for example, to find levels that are too hard) we use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They collect anonymous information such as which levels were started, finished or failed, hints used, whether an optional video was watched, in-app purchase events, device model and Android version, crash reports, approximate country, and a random app instance ID. This data is not linked to your name or email and is never used to personalise ads. It is processed by Google according to the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase privacy information. You can switch it off at any time in the game: Settings → "Share anonymous usage stats and crash reports".

Advertising

Levels 1 to 7 show no ads at all. From level 8 the game can show a small banner on menu screens and rewarded videos that you choose to watch (for a hint, tokens or a second chance). There are no forced full-screen pop-up ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location (from the IP address), app interactions, and crash and diagnostic data, according to the Google Privacy Policy. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice in Settings → Change ad consent, or in your Android settings.

In-app purchases

Optional token packs are sold through Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game only receives confirmation of a completed purchase so it can add the tokens.

Backup

If Android backup is turned on, Android may save a copy of the game data listed above (progress, tokens, settings) to your Google account, or copy it to a new phone during setup, so you can continue playing. Nothing else is included. You control this in your Android settings.

Children

This game is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to general-audience content.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset progress in the game, or uninstall the game. You can also email us with any request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
