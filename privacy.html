Privacy Policy

Color Puzzle World (Android package com.shafique.joki) · Last updated: September 24, 2026

Summary

Color Puzzle World is an offline puzzle game. We do not have accounts or a server, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. The only third parties are Google AdMob (ads) and Google Play (the optional Remove Ads purchase).

Data stored on your device

The game saves the following only on your phone, so you can continue playing:

  • level progress, stars, scores, moves and times
  • coins, boosters, chests, collection items (ball skins, tubes, themes), achievements and event badges
  • daily and weekly puzzle results, monthly challenge progress and your login streak
  • settings (sound, music, vibration, reminder, symbols, effects quality, Zen sound volume)

This data is not sent to us.

Advertising

This free game shows ads from Google AdMob. To show, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location (from the IP address), app interactions, and crash and diagnostic data. This information is handled by Google according to the Google Privacy Policy. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Privacy screen (Change ad consent) or in your Android settings.

Watching a rewarded ad is always optional.

In-app purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game only receives whether the purchase is active, so ads can be turned off and the purchase can be restored on a new phone.

Backup

If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may save a copy of the game's data to your Google account so it can be restored on a new device. You control this in your Android settings.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the game schedules a local notification on your phone. No data is sent anywhere for this.

Children

This game is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to general-audience content.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset progress in the game, or uninstall the game, to delete what is stored on your device. To delete a backup copy, remove it in Android settings (Google → Backup). You can also email us with any request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page and shown in the game.
