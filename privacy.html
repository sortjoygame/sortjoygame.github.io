Privacy Policy

Sort Joy: Water Sort Puzzle (Android package: com.shafique.joki)
Last updated: September 23, 2026

Introduction

Sort Joy: Water Sort Puzzle ("the app") is a free puzzle game published by AlFalah Badin Sindh. This page explains what information is used when you play.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location, and you do not need an account to play. We do not run our own servers.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, coins, stars, themes, streak and settings) is saved only on your phone.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device advertising ID, IP address and general app usage to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), you will be asked for consent first, and you can change your choice later. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

In-app purchases

Purchases such as "Remove Ads" are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Third-party services

Google AdMob and Google Play have their own privacy policies:
https://policies.google.com/privacy
https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Children's privacy

This app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Open Settings in the app and tap "Reset progress / delete my data", or simply uninstall the app. This removes all game data stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The new version will be posted on this page.
