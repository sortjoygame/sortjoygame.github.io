Privacy Policy

Color Puzzle World (Android package com.shafique.joki) · Last updated: September 24, 2026

Data we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address or any account. Your game progress, stars, scores and settings are stored only on your device.

Advertising

This free game shows ads from Google AdMob. To show, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location (from the IP address), app interactions, and crash and diagnostic data. This information is handled by Google according to the Google Privacy Policy. You can change your ad choices in the consent form shown in the game, in the game's Privacy screen, or in your Android settings.

Children

This game is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to general-audience content.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset progress in the game, or uninstall the game, to delete everything stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
